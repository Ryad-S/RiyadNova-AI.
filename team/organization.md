# RiyadNova AI Organization Structure

## Overview

This document defines the organizational structure of RiyadNova AI, including company departments, key roles, responsibilities, and future team expansion.

The goal is to build a strong team capable of developing, operating, and scaling a global AI company.

---

# Founder

## Founder Role

The founder is responsible for:

- Company vision.
- Strategic decisions.
- Product direction.
- Partnerships.
- Long-term growth.

---

# Executive Team

## 1. Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Responsibilities:

- Lead company strategy.
- Manage business growth.
- Build partnerships.
- Represent the company.

---

## 2. Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Responsibilities:

- Lead technology development.
- Manage engineering teams.
- Define technical architecture.
- Oversee AI infrastructure.

---

## 3. Chief Product Officer (CPO)

Responsibilities:

- Manage product development.
- Understand user needs.
- Define product roadmap.
- Improve user experience.

---
