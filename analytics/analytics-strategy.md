# RiyadNova AI Analytics Strategy

## Overview

This document defines the analytics strategy of RiyadNova AI.

Analytics helps the company understand user behavior, measure platform performance, improve AI services, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

# Analytics Goals

The main objectives are:

- Understand user behavior.
- Measure product performance.
- Improve user experience.
- Track business growth.
- Optimize AI features.

---

# Data Categories

RiyadNova AI collects and analyzes:

## 1. User Analytics

Tracks:

- Number of users.
- New registrations.
- Active users.
- User retention.
- User engagement.

---

## 2. Product Analytics

Measures:

- Feature usage.
- Most popular AI tools.
- User journey.
- Platform interactions.

---

## 3. AI Performance Analytics

Tracks:

- AI response quality.
- User satisfaction.
- AI agent performance.
- Processing time.

---

## 4. Business Analytics

Measures:

- Subscription growth.
- Revenue performance.
- Conversion rates.
- Customer value.

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## User KPIs

- Monthly Active Users (MAU).
- Daily Active Users (DAU).
- User retention rate.
- Registration growth.

---

## Product KPIs

- Number of AI requests.
- Completed projects.
- Generated business plans.
- Feature adoption rate.

---

## Business KPIs

- Subscription revenue.
- Premium users.
- Customer acquisition cost.
- Customer lifetime value.

---

# Analytics Tools

Possible technologies:

- Google Analytics.
- Product analytics platforms.
- Database analytics.
- Custom dashboards.

---

# Data Visualization

The platform will provide dashboards showing:

- User growth.
- AI usage.
- Business performance.
- System health.

---

# Data Privacy

Analytics systems must ensure:

- User privacy protection.
- Secure data storage.
- Limited data access.
- Compliance with regulations.

---

# Future Analytics Expansion

Future improvements:

- Predictive analytics.
- AI-powered business insights.
- Automated reports.
- Advanced user behavior analysis.

---

# Analytics Goal

Build a data-driven platform that continuously improves RiyadNova AI and delivers better value to entrepreneurs worldwide.
