# RiyadNova AI Logo Guidelines

## Logo Concept

The logo combines:

- The letter R from Riyad.
- AI neural network patterns.
- A growth symbol representing progress.
- A Nova element representing innovation.

---

## Logo Style

The logo should be:

- Minimal
- Modern
- Premium
- Futuristic
- Easy to recognize

---

## Logo Versions

Primary Logo:
Full color logo with company name.

Dark Version:
For dark backgrounds.

Light Version:
For light backgrounds.

Icon Only:
Used for app icon and social media.

---

## Logo Usage Rules

Do not:

- Stretch the logo.
- Change colors randomly.
- Add unnecessary effects.
- Modify proportions.
