# RiyadNova AI Design System

## Overview

This document defines the visual design system of RiyadNova AI.

The design system ensures a consistent, modern, and professional user experience across the website, dashboard, and future mobile applications.

---

# Design Philosophy

RiyadNova AI follows a design philosophy based on:

- Intelligence.
- Simplicity.
- Innovation.
- Trust.
- Professional technology.

The interface should feel like a modern AI business partner.

---
