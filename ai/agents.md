# RiyadNova AI Agents System

## Overview

RiyadNova AI is powered by a multi-agent artificial intelligence system.

Each AI agent specializes in a specific business function and works together to provide users with a complete digital co-founder experience.

---

# 1. AI Idea Analyzer Agent

## Purpose

Analyze business ideas and determine their potential before users invest time and money.

## Responsibilities

- Understand business ideas.
- Evaluate market opportunities.
- Identify strengths and weaknesses.
- Detect possible risks.
- Provide improvement suggestions.

## Input

- Business idea description
- Industry
- Target customers
- Location

## Output

- Opportunity score
- Risk analysis
- Recommendations
- Next steps

---
