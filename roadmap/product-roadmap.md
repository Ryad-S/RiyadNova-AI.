# RiyadNova AI Product Roadmap

## Overview

This document defines the development roadmap of RiyadNova AI.

The roadmap describes the evolution of the platform from the first MVP version into a global AI-powered entrepreneurship ecosystem.

---

# Phase 1: MVP Development

## Goal

Build the first functional version of RiyadNova AI and validate the idea with early users.

## Core Features

### AI Idea Analyzer

- Analyze business ideas.
- Evaluate opportunities.
- Identify risks.
- Provide recommendations.

### User Accounts

- Registration.
- Login.
- User profiles.

### Project Workspace

- Create business projects.
- Save ideas.
- Track progress.

### Basic AI Co-Founder

- Answer business questions.
- Provide guidance.
- Suggest next steps.

---

# Phase 2: Beta Launch

## Goal

Release the platform to selected users and collect feedback.

## Improvements

- Better AI responses.
- Improved user interface.
- More business templates.
- User feedback system.

## Beta Users

- Entrepreneurs.
- Students.
- Startup founders.
- Small business owners.

---

# Phase 3: Platform Expansion

## Goal

Transform RiyadNova AI into a complete business creation platform.

## New Features

### Advanced Market Research

- Competitor analysis.
- Industry trends.
- Customer insights.

### Business Plan Generator

Creates:

- Executive summary.
- Business model.
- Marketing plan.
- Financial overview.

### AI Business Agents

Add specialized agents:

- Marketing Agent.
- Finance Agent.
- Sales Agent.
- Legal Assistant.
