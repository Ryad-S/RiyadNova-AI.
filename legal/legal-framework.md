# RiyadNova AI Legal Framework

## Overview

This document defines the legal foundation required for RiyadNova AI to operate as a professional technology company.

It covers user protection, privacy, platform rules, intellectual property, and legal responsibilities.

---

# 1. Terms of Service

## Purpose

Define the rules and conditions for using RiyadNova AI.

Users agree to:

- Provide accurate information.
- Use the platform responsibly.
- Respect intellectual property rights.
- Follow platform policies.

---

# 2. Privacy Policy

## Purpose

Explain how user information is collected, stored, and protected.

RiyadNova AI protects:

- User accounts.
- Business ideas.
- Project information.
- AI conversations.
- Personal data.

---

# 3. Data Protection

The platform must implement:

- Data encryption.
- Secure authentication.
- Access control.
- Regular security reviews.

---

# 4. Intellectual Property

RiyadNova AI owns:

- Platform software.
- Brand identity.
- AI workflows.
- Design systems.
- Original content.

Users maintain ownership of their business ideas and submitted information.

---

# 5. AI Usage Policy

## AI Limitations

Users should understand that:

- AI recommendations are suggestions.
- Users should validate important business decisions.
- AI does not replace professional legal, financial, or business advisors.

---

# 6. User Responsibilities

Users must not:

- Abuse platform services.
- Attempt unauthorized access.
- Upload harmful content.
- Violate applicable laws.

---

# 7. Security Responsibilities

RiyadNova AI will:

- Protect user information.
- Monitor security risks.
- Improve system safety.
- Respond to security issues.

---

# 8. Subscription and Payments

The platform may provide:

- Free plans.

- Business subscriptions.

Payment policies include:

- Billing terms.
- Cancellation rules.
- Subscription management.
