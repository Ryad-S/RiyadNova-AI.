# RiyadNova AI Marketing Strategy

## Overview

This document defines the marketing strategy for RiyadNova AI.

The goal is to build brand awareness, attract entrepreneurs, acquire early users, and establish RiyadNova AI as a leading AI business partner platform.

---

# Marketing Objectives

The main objectives are:

- Build brand recognition.
- Attract entrepreneurs and startups.
- Generate platform users.
- Create a strong online community.
- Convert users into paying customers.

---

# Target Audience

## Primary Users

### Entrepreneurs

People who want to:

- Validate business ideas.
- Build startups.
- Create business plans.
- Understand markets.

---

### Small Business Owners

People who need:

- Business improvement strategies.
- Marketing support.
- Growth recommendations.

---

### Students

People interested in:

- Entrepreneurship.
- Innovation.
- Startup development.
