# RiyadNova AI Business Model

## Overview

This document defines the business model of RiyadNova AI.

The goal is to create a sustainable AI-powered platform that helps entrepreneurs transform ideas into successful businesses while generating long-term revenue.

---

# Business Model Type

RiyadNova AI follows a Software as a Service (SaaS) business model.

Users access the platform online and pay for advanced AI-powered business features.

---

# Value Proposition

RiyadNova AI provides:

- AI-powered business analysis.
- Market research.
- Business planning.
- Financial guidance.
- Marketing strategies.
- AI Co-Founder support.

The platform reduces the complexity of starting and growing a business.
