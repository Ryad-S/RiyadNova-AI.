# RiyadNova AI UI/UX Specification

## 1. Design Philosophy

RiyadNova AI interface should provide a premium, futuristic, and simple user experience.

The design should make users feel they have access to an intelligent AI business partner.

Main principles:

- Simple navigation
- Clear information
- Professional appearance
- Fast interaction
- AI-driven experience

---

# 2. Website Pages

## Landing Page

Purpose:

Introduce RiyadNova AI and explain the platform value.

Sections:

### Hero Section

Contains:

- Company logo
- Main headline
- Short description
- Call-to-action buttons

Headline:

"Transform Your Ideas Into Successful Businesses With AI"

Buttons:

- Start Your Journey
- Explore Features


---

## Features Section

Displays AI capabilities:

- Idea Analysis
- Market Research
- Business Planning
- Financial Strategy
- Marketing AI
- AI Co-Founder


---

## How It Works

Steps:

1. Enter your business idea
2. AI analyzes the opportunity
3. Generate business strategy
4. Start building your company
