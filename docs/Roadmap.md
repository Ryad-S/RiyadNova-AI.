# RiyadNova AI Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation & MVP Development

Timeline: 0 - 6 Months

Goals:
- Build the first working version of RiyadNova AI.
- Validate the product idea with early users.
- Create the core AI business analysis system.

Key Features:
- AI Idea Analyzer
- Basic Market Research
- Business Plan Generator
- User Dashboard
- AI Co-Founder Chat

Objectives:
- Test user needs.
- Collect feedback.
- Improve AI responses.
- Build a strong technical foundation.

---

## Phase 2: Beta Launch

Timeline: 6 - 12 Months

Goals:
- Launch the platform to a wider audience.
- Improve user experience.
- Introduce premium features.

Key Features:
- Advanced Market Analysis
- Financial Planning Tools
- Marketing Strategy Generator
- Project Tracking
- User Accounts and Subscriptions

Objectives:
- Acquire first customers.
- Build community.
- Establish revenue model.

---

## Phase 3: Growth & Expansion

Timeline: 1 - 3 Years

Goals:
- Expand globally.
- Build advanced AI capabilities.

Key Features:
- AI Business Agents
- Investor Matching
- Automated Reports
- Team Collaboration
- Mobile Application

Objectives:
- Reach international markets.
- Partner with organizations.
- Increase platform adoption.

---

## Phase 4: Global AI Entrepreneurship Ecosystem

Timeline: 3 - 5 Years

Goals:
- Become a global AI platform for entrepreneurship.

Future Capabilities:
- AI employees
- Business automation
- Startup ecosystem
- Global entrepreneur network
- AI-powered company building

Vision:
RiyadNova AI becomes the operating system for creating and growing businesses.
